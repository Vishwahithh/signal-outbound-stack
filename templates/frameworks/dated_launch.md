# dated_launch: one dated event, then the decision it creates

Status: blind review winner. Not yet sent at volume.
Use for: email one, when the company launched, priced, raised, hired or announced something with a date in the last
90 days.

```
Hi {first name},

{one sentence: the event with its month, one product name at most, no second fact, no counting their things}

{one question: the specific decision this event puts on their desk, phrased so only their company could be asked it}

{one sentence: who the sender is, verbatim from the sender lines in the config}

{one sentence ask tied to the question, answerable with one word}

{sender first name}
```

Must not: say "congrats", describe how they price unless the research states it, or use a number the reader cannot
place from the email itself.
