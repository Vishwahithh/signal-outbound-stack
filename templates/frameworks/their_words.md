# their_words: their own sentence, then the question it raises

Status: blind review winner (5 rounds, fact-checked, top score each round). Not yet sent at volume.
Use for: email one, when the person posted or spoke publicly in the last 90 days.

```
Hi {first name},

{one sentence: "You wrote in <month> that" followed by a short quote of their own words, copied exactly from the
research, under 20 words}

{one or two sentences: the decision their own statement implies for their company, asked as one question only they
can answer; do not assert the answer}

{one sentence: who the sender is, verbatim from the sender lines in the config}

{one sentence ask tied to the question, answerable with one word, e.g. "Reply yes and I'll send a one-page sketch of
how I would set it."}

{sender first name}
```

Must not: quote anything not in the research, change their words, join the quote to a launch unless both dates say
they are linked, or name more than one product.
