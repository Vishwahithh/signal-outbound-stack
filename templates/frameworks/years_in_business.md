# years_in_business: how long they have been trading, then the pricing question age raises

Status: UNTESTED. Candidate for owner-run service firms where no dated event exists. Test against written_outline
before relying on it.
Use for: email one, firms 8 or more years old, from the company register or their own site.

```
Hi {first name},

{one sentence: the year they started and what they do, from the company register or their own site, e.g. "<Company>
has been doing <what they do> since <year>."}

{one question: whether the way they price today was set when they started or has changed since; ask, never assume}

{one sentence: who the sender is, verbatim from the sender lines in the config}

{one sentence: offer a short written outline for their firm, answerable with one word}

{sender first name}
```

Must not: say or imply their prices are out of date, use "the same old" or "never revisited", or guess the year.
