# last_note: close the loop in one line

Status: real data says third touches produced no positive replies in any campaign we ran (hundreds sent). Keep it to
one line, or end the sequence after email two and spend the send on a new person.
Use for: final touch, only if you keep one.

```
{first name},

{one line: say you will leave it there and the offer stands if it becomes relevant; no guilt, no "just bumping"}

{sender first name}
```
