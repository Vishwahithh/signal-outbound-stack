# written_outline: one checked fact, then a free written outline, answerable with one word

Status: real replies. This shape produced every positive reply recorded at volume (11 yeses across about 2,300
contacts). It worked even with weaker copy around it, because the ask is a free thing about their own company.
Use for: email one, any segment. Default framework.

```
Hi {first name},

{one sentence: a fact about what their company does or recently did, taken from their own site or a dated source,
plain words, no praise, no adjectives about them}

{one sentence: who the sender is, taken verbatim from the sender lines in the config, never a sector the sender has
no client in}

{one sentence: offer a short written outline of how the sender would look at this topic for their company, free and
with no obligation, naming the company}

{one line the reader can answer with one word, e.g. "Open to a reply and I'll send it over?"}

{sender first name}
```

Must not: diagnose them ("leaving money on the table", "set years ago"), invent a result, add "if useful" or "if it
helps" before the offer, or ask for a call in this email.
