# their_desk: the part of the decision this person actually owns

Status: blind review, built on the rule "write to the person's desk". Not yet sent at volume.
Use for: email one, to someone who shares the decision or does not own it (finance, product, sales leaders).

```
Hi {first name},

{one sentence: a dated fact about their company that touches their role}

{one question about the part of the decision on their desk: for finance, margin or the price ladder; for product,
which plan a new feature goes in; for sales, what goes on the quote or the renewal}

{one sentence: who the sender is, verbatim from the sender lines in the config}

{one sentence ask: either the one-word ask tied to the question, or, once and plainly, who owns the pricing call}

{sender first name}
```

Must not: ask a finance lead about packaging or a product lead about margin, or pretend they own a decision they do not.
