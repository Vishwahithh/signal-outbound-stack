# Voice-of-customer phrase bank (example format)

Copy to config/voc_bank.md (git-ignored) and fill it with real, verbatim lines for YOUR client's buyers.

How to build one: have research agents collect 300-500 public quotes from the last two years (podcasts, interviews,
the buyers' own LinkedIn posts, forums, reviews) where people in the target personas talk about the problem the
client's offer solves. Keep each quote verbatim with role, year and source type, tag it with a topic, then keep the
10-15 most telling lines per persona here. Prefer companies of the target size.

USE: borrow the framing, the worry and the vocabulary. NEVER quote these to a prospect, never attribute them to the
prospect, never say "other CFOs tell us".
